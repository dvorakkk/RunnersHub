Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 September 2026

Short version: RunnersHub is a running-route platform. We process information needed to publish and display routes, support likes/comments, provide nearby-route features, manage GPX downloads and sharing unlocks, and operate the website. We do not ask users to create a Google account to use RunnersHub.

This Privacy Policy explains how RunnersHub (“RunnersHub”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) handles information when you visit or use the RunnersHub website. By using the service, you acknowledge the practices described below.

1. Information we process

Route and GPX information

When you publish a route, RunnersHub may process the information you provide or that is extracted from the uploaded GPX file, including route name, route type, distance, elevation, route coordinates, geographic area, route details, timestamps, a GPX file identifier, and technical route metadata such as a file hash. The published route information may be visible to other visitors.

Comments

If you post a comment, we store the display name you provide (or “Anonymous” when none is provided), the comment text, the related route, and the submission time. Comments are public on the relevant route.

Likes and anonymous identifiers

RunnersHub uses an anonymous browser identifier stored in your browser’s local storage to associate likes and certain route-unlock/reward actions with the same browser. This identifier is not presented as your real name or email address.

Nearby routes

If you choose the nearby-route feature and grant your browser permission to use location, your browser supplies latitude and longitude to RunnersHub so the service can calculate which published routes are within the selected radius. The feature is user-initiated. RunnersHub does not need your location for ordinary browsing.

Share verification

When you use share-to-unlock, you may submit a public post URL from a supported platform. RunnersHub stores the selected platform, the submitted URL (subject to length limits), the related route, the anonymous runner identifier, and the time/day of the unlock event.

Technical information

Requests to the backend may be associated with technical request information such as an IP address or forwarded client address for rate limiting and abuse prevention. The application uses this information in short-lived server-side rate-limit buckets rather than as a public user profile.

2. How we use information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve route discovery and sharing features.
  • Store and publish route, comment, and like data submitted through the service.
  • Process GPX uploads and make eligible GPX files available for download.
  • Provide nearby-route calculations when you request them.
  • Operate share-to-unlock and, when enabled, RunnersHub rewards/points features.
  • Prevent abuse, enforce request limits, troubleshoot errors, and maintain service security.
  • Display advertising where enabled.

3. Google services and storage

RunnersHub’s backend uses Google APIs to operate its data store and file storage. Depending on the current deployment, route and application data may be stored in Google Sheets and GPX files may be stored in Google Drive under the RunnersHub operator’s Google account. These credentials are server-side and are not exposed to visitors through the website.

RunnersHub does not use Google Sign-In as a requirement for ordinary visitors. The Google account used by the backend is an operator-side infrastructure account, not a user account created by RunnersHub.

4. Advertising

RunnersHub may display Google AdSense advertising. Advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and applicable consent requirements. Your browser or privacy tools may block advertising scripts; doing so does not change the core route functionality.

5. When information is shared

Published routes, public comments, likes/counts, and information you intentionally make public through sharing features may be visible to other visitors. We do not sell personal information as a business model. Information may also be processed by service providers and infrastructure platforms necessary to operate the website, including hosting and Google services.

6. Retention and deletion

Published route, comment, like, and unlock/reward records may remain in the application’s data store while the service is operating. Retention depends on the operational needs of RunnersHub and its underlying storage. Some records may need to be retained for integrity, abuse prevention, or audit purposes.

If you need a specific public comment or route-related record reviewed or removed, contact us with enough information to identify it. We may ask for reasonable verification before making changes.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical measures appropriate to a small web application, including server-side handling of Google credentials, HTTPS when provided by the hosting platform, request validation, and rate limiting. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

RunnersHub is a general-purpose running-route website and is not specifically directed at children. Do not submit unnecessary personal information, and parents or guardians should supervise use where appropriate.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the service, data practices, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, contact the RunnersHub operator at gawembengi@gmail.com.
